The nuclear medicine technologist behind Advanced Imaging's SPECT/CT, PET/CT and nuclear medicine services.

Nuclear Medicine Technologist

About Me

I'm Dean Kefalas, a nuclear medicine technologist and the founder of Advanced Imaging. I trained in Loma Linda University's Nuclear Medicine Technology Program and completed clinical rotations at multiple sites, working hands-on with the same kinds of systems hospitals and imaging clinics depend on every day.

That clinical background shapes how I help facilities. I understand what technologists need at the scanner, what physicians need in the images, and what administrators need from the investment. My goal is to help every hospital and imaging clinic choose and run equipment that delivers better answers for their patients.

Experience

Nuclear Medicine Clinical Internship Experience

As part of my training, I completed a nuclear medicine clinical internship with rotations at multiple clinical sites. Working alongside experienced technologists and physicians, I gained hands-on experience across the daily work of a nuclear medicine department:

  • Patient preparation, positioning and care during imaging procedures
  • Performing nuclear medicine imaging studies under clinical supervision
  • Radiopharmaceutical handling with radiation safety practices
  • Daily equipment quality control and department workflow
