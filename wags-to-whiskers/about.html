About Wags to Whiskers

Caring, Experienced Pet Groomers in Long Beach

We're pet lovers first. Wags to Whiskers was founded on a simple idea: every dog and cat deserves to be treated like family, and every pet parent deserves to know exactly what to expect.

Our Story

From Wags to Whiskers, Every Pet Is Family

Our grooming salon was built for pets who need a little extra patience and for owners who want a groomer they can trust. We take time to get to know each pet's personality, health and preferences, then tailor every groom to them.

Whether it's your puppy's very first bath or your senior cat's monthly trim, you can count on gentle hands, honest advice and a clean, calm space.
