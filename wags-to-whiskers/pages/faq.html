Frequently Asked Questions About Dog & Cat Grooming

Booking & Your Free Consultation

What happens at the free grooming consultation?

You and your pet meet your groomer, we check coat, skin, nails and ears, talk about the look you want and any sensitivities, and give you a grooming plan with an exact price. It takes about 15 minutes and there's no obligation to book.

How do I book a grooming appointment?

Request your free consultation using the form on our booking page or call us at {{phone}}. We'll confirm a time that suits you, usually within one business day.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Full grooms are by appointment so every pet gets one-on-one attention. Quick services like nail trims may be available as walk-ins during business hours. Call ahead to check.

Pricing & Timing

How much does dog grooming cost?

Pricing depends on your pet's breed, size, coat condition and the services you choose. That's why we give an exact quote at your free consultation, so you know the price before any work begins.

How long does a grooming appointment take?

Most full grooms take two to four hours depending on size, coat and temperament. We'll give you an estimated pick-up time at drop-off and contact you when your pet is ready.

How often should my dog be groomed?

Most dogs benefit from a full groom every four to eight weeks. Long or curly coats such as doodles and poodles usually need grooming every four to six weeks, while short-coated breeds can often go longer with baths in between.

Pet Health & Safety

What vaccinations does my pet need?

For the safety of every pet in our salon we ask for current rabies vaccination records, and for dogs we recommend DHPP and Bordetella. Please bring records or have your vet send them before your first appointment.

My pet is anxious or older. Can you still groom them?

Yes. We use fear-free, low-stress handling, take breaks when needed and never force a pet through a groom. Let us know about anxiety, health issues or past bad experiences at your consultation so we can plan around them.

What if my pet's coat is badly matted?

Severe matting can be painful to brush out, so for your pet's comfort we may recommend a short, humane shave-down and a fresh start. We'll always discuss options with you first.

Do you groom cats?

Yes. We offer gentle cat grooming including baths, de-matting, lion cuts and nail trims, handled patiently in a calm environment.
