What is the difference between SPECT/CT and PET/CT?

Both are hybrid imaging methods that combine functional nuclear imaging with CT anatomy. SPECT/CT uses gamma-emitting tracers and is widely used for cardiac, bone, thyroid and general nuclear medicine studies. PET/CT uses positron-emitting tracers and is central to oncology staging and treatment response, as well as cardiac and neurological imaging.

Should we upgrade our existing gamma camera or replace it?

It depends on the age and support status of your system, your study volumes, and your clinical goals. We compare upgrade paths against replacement options, including total cost of ownership, so you can make the right call for your department.
